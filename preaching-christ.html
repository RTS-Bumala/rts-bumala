Preaching Christ from Every Text, Every Time

A seminary-level course in Christ-centered, gospel-driven preaching using Timothy Keller’s Preaching as a primary guide.

Course Vision

This course equips future pastors and teachers to proclaim Jesus Christ faithfully from the whole counsel of Scripture. Students will learn to avoid moralism and abstraction, and instead preach sermons that are text-driven, Christ-exalting, pastorally sensitive, and culturally aware.

“All Christian preaching is preaching Christ.” — Timothy Keller

Learning Objectives

  • Preach Christ faithfully from every biblical genre
  • Integrate exegesis, biblical theology, and gospel application
  • Avoid moralism while preaching obedience grounded in grace
  • Address both believers and skeptics in every sermon

Course Outline

Week 1 – The Nature and Power of Preaching

Why preaching is central to God’s redemptive work and how authority flows from Scripture to proclamation.

Week 2 – Scripture as One Redemptive Story

Creation, Fall, Redemption, and Consummation with Christ as the hermeneutical key (Luke 24).

Week 3 – Preaching Christ from Narrative

Typology, redemptive tension, and avoiding “be like David” moralism.

Week 4 – Preaching Christ from the Law

Law and gospel distinction; Christ as law-fulfiller and curse-bearer.

Week 5 – Preaching Christ from Wisdom Literature

Christ as the wisdom of God and the answer to suffering and perplexity.

Week 6–11 – Prophets, Psalms, Epistles, Application, and Pastoral Identity

Christ-centered preaching across genres with gospel-driven application.

Required Texts

  • Timothy Keller, Preaching: Communicating Faith in an Age of Skepticism
  • Sidney Greidanus, Preaching Christ from the Old Testament
  • Graeme Goldsworthy, According to Plan

Assessment

  • Christ-centered sermon manuscript (Old or New Testament)
  • In-class sermon delivery
  • Integrative theological reflection paper
